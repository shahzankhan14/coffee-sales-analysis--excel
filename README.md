# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis (Excel)
## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes **149,000+ sales transactions** from a coffee shop chain across three store locations in New York (Astoria, Hell's Kitchen, and Lower Manhattan) to identify revenue drivers, peak operating hours, and top-performing product categories.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem
The management team wants to understand customer purchasing behavior, store-level performance, and product profitability to optimize inventory, staffing, and promotional strategies.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
* **Tool:** Microsoft Excel
* **Techniques Used:** Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Calculated Columns, Data Cleaning, Dashboarding

---

## 📊 Key Findings & Insights
* 💰 **Total Revenue:** Generated **$1,397,624.66** across **149,116 transactions**.
* ☕ **Top Revenue Categories:** **Coffee** ($269,952) and **Tea** ($196,405) represent the highest sales volume and revenue.
* 📍 **Location Breakdown:** Revenue is evenly distributed across locations, led by **Hell's Kitchen ($236.5K)**, **Astoria ($232.2K)**, and **Lower Manhattan ($230.1K)**.
* 🥐 **Cross-Selling Potential:** **Bakery** items sold 23,214 units, showing strong potential for morning bundle promotions with hot beverages.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
1. **Morning Bundling:** Create coffee + bakery combo offers during morning peak hours to increase average order value.
2. **Inventory Optimization:** Ensure high stock availability for Coffee and Tea ingredients at all store locations.
3. **Staff Allocation:** Align staff scheduling with peak transaction hours identified in the hourly analysis.
